Applications

Artistic experiences for businesses

KIBART designs artistic interventions tailored to professional contexts. These experiences create unifying moments for teams and memorable ones for guests.

Live art

With Live Art, art becomes a moment of sharing within the company. Through artistic performances and creations made on site, teams come together around a collective experience that fosters exchange, creativity and cohesion.

This dedicated moment allows team members to step outside their usual work environment and share an inspiring experience where everyone can observe, participate and connect differently.

✕ Close

Artistic events

KIBART designs artistic events that transform a venue or a gathering into a true experience.

Artists, creative performances, and visual installations create a unique and memorable atmosphere for your teams or guests.

These experiences can also be enhanced with aerial filming and staging using drones, to document the event and produce impactful visual content.

✕ Close

Space enhancement

Art can transform a space and give it an identity. In offices, shops or reception areas, the presence of artworks creates a more vibrant atmosphere and reflects a sensitivity to creativity and aesthetics.

Artworks can be selected and renewed to accompany the evolution of a space, enrich the visitor experience and bring an artistic dimension to professional or personal environments.
