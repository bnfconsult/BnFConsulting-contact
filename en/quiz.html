The digital audit, a strategic lever

Not an inspection. A decision.

Optimizing blindly? The BnF audit is not a critique: it's the strategic reading that reveals barriers and opportunities before taking action.

You can't optimize what you don't understand.

What the BnF audit reveals

Hidden inconsistencies, attention loss, conversion barriers, image/reality gaps, underused levers. BnF Strategy pillar: Mandatory global diagnosis.

The 4 key BnF dimensions

  • A. Readability: Clear promise? Structured offer?

Risk without an audit

  • Avoid unnecessary spending.
  • Structure before automating.
