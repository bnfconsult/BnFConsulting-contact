Our approach

Many businesses already have the resources they need to accelerate. The real challenge is to identify the under-used levers and turn them into tangible advantages.

Our method

Understand first. Then act.

Every engagement follows the same path. This is not a technical service: it is a capacity for judgement and orchestration.

01

Understand

Meet the business leader. Understand their company, market, objectives, difficulties and ambitions.

02

Diagnose

Identify untapped opportunities, friction points, inconsistencies and real priorities.

03

Build a trajectory

Determine what needs to be done, in what order, with which resources and which indicators.

04

Mobilise

Bring in the expertise that is needed, and only that, at the moment it is useful.

05

Steer

Keep the overall vision of the project. Coordinate the contributors, follow up on decisions, maintain consistency.

06

Measure and adjust

Observe the results, identify what works, correct what needs correcting, keep developing.

What you receive

Three packages. One point of contact.

Depending on your need and the agreed scope, our support takes one of these three forms. In every case, you know in advance what you will receive.

Package 01

Diagnosis & roadmap

“I want to know what to improve and where to start.”

What you receive
  • In-depth conversation
  • Analysis of the current situation
  • Reasoned priorities
  • 90-day action plan
  • Debrief

Billing: fixed fee, depending on the scope analysed.

Package 02

Design & deployment

“I have a goal and I want the project to take shape.”

What you receive
  • Targeted diagnosis
  • Proposed solution
  • Budget and schedule
  • Mobilisation of experts
  • Coordination and delivery of the agreed programme

Billing: quote per project, with payment milestones.

Package 03

Steering & growth

“I want to move forward over time with one person who follows everything.”

What you receive
  • Updated roadmap
  • Coordination of actions
  • Dashboard
  • Monthly review
  • Recommendations for the next steps

Billing: monthly fee; productions and external purchases quoted separately.

The first conversation is used to choose the right package and define its scope.
