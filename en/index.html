The digital audit, a strategic lever

Not a check. A decision.

Optimizing blind? The BnF audit is not a critique — it's the strategic reading that reveals obstacles and opportunities before acting.

We don't optimize what we don't understand.

What the BnF audit reveals

Hidden inconsistencies, attention losses, conversion friction, image/reality gaps, underused levers. BnF Strategy pillar: mandatory global diagnosis.

The 4 key BnF dimensions

  • A. Readability: Clear promise? Structured offer?
  • B. Coherence: Alignment site/Google/social/reality?
  • C. Performance: Smooth journey or friction?
  • D. Data: Do KPIs actually drive decisions? (BnF Data & Steering pillar)

The risk without an audit

Stacking tools on an unstable base = amplifying errors. Investing without structure = waste. Systemic BnF logic: foundations first.

What BnF activates concretely

  • Clarify your trajectory.
  • Prioritize max ROI.
  • Avoid unnecessary spending.
  • Structure before automating.

The BnF audit = entry into the ecosystem: Clarity → Coherence → Sustainable growth.
